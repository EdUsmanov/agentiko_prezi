# Open development corpus inventory

Suites contain 4 core cases, 9 extended cases, and 24 development cases.

## Original external references and replay inputs

The original suite registers 9 cases and 8 distinct external template hashes. Replay uses simplified seeded analogs: 9 files, 7 byte-unique files, and 4 aspect/column/theme profiles.

Exact repeated source hashes and generated replay-analog hashes are listed separately in the JSON inventory. A shared source host means related branding provenance; it does not mean identical template bytes.

## Owned development fixtures

Revision 2026-09-29-development.4 contains 15 independently authored source tasks, 15 distinct native template files, 3 distinct screenshot assets, 15 declared families, and 15 measurable source-slide signatures.

Structural signatures omit text, names, color, fonts, unused layouts, master graphics, and empty non-placeholder text boxes; they retain canvas size, top-level source-slide shape types and bounds, native table dimensions, and chart types. A structural signature is a measurable fixture fingerprint, not a certification of semantic or visual design independence.

Every fixture is original CC0-licensed synthetic material. Ledgers are silver and unreviewed, with each quote anchored to its task source. The replay analogs do not reproduce WPI reference pages; those pages remain an original-template reference constraint only.

See `inventory_report()` or `python -m audit_e2e inventory` for per-case IDs, SHA-256 values, feature checks, family/material IDs, source evidence, image dimensions, exact duplicates, and structural signature groups.

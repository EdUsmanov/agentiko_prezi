# Workshop Attendance Measures
## Overview
This note defines fictional workshop measures with units and source labels.
## Purpose
This note defines a small set of fictional measures for a volunteer workshop. Each value includes its unit and source label so the terms remain distinguishable.
## Attendance
The check-in sheet `WS-17` records 84 visits during May 2027. This is a visit count, not a count of unique people.
## Completion
The facilitator log `WS-18` records 61 completed repair jobs in May 2027. A visit may include more than one job.
## Wait time
The queue sample `WS-19` reports a median wait of 23 minutes across 40 sampled visits. The sample covers only Saturday sessions.
## Return visits
The follow-up sheet `WS-20` records 14 visitors who returned within thirty days. The sheet does not include visitors who declined follow-up.
## Interpretation limit
These four records use different units and collection methods. They should not be combined into one rate without a matched denominator and a defined counting rule.

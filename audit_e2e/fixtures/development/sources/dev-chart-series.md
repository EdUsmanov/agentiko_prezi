# Rooftop Garden Trial Readings
## Overview
Five months of fictional garden readings are supplied for chart-label testing.
## Source note
The values below are fictional monthly readings from a demonstration rooftop garden. They are supplied to test chart labels and do not represent measured production.
## Leaf mass series
The recorded leaf mass is 4.2 kg in April, 5.1 kg in May, 6.0 kg in June, 5.8 kg in July, and 4.9 kg in August.
## Water series
Water use is 310 L in April, 360 L in May, 420 L in June, 455 L in July, and 390 L in August.
## Temperature context
The monthly mean temperature entries are 12 °C, 16 °C, 20 °C, 23 °C, and 21 °C from April through August.
## Unit warning
Leaf mass, water use, and temperature use different units and scales. A single unlabelled vertical axis would make the series ambiguous.
## Source labels
Leaf mass comes from demo sheet G-01, water from G-02, and temperature from G-03. The sheets are generated fixture data rather than external measurements.

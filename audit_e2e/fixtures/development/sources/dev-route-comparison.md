# Mobile Library Route Options
## Overview
The county is comparing three routes for a six-month mobile library trial.
## Decision context
The source team has not selected a route and has not estimated demand outside the listed stops.
## North Loop
The North Loop covers 18 kilometres and visits four stops. It needs 3.5 driver hours per service day. Its two rural stops have no indoor waiting area.
## Market Link
The Market Link covers 12 kilometres and visits five stops. It needs 4.0 driver hours because the central market stop has a scheduled 45-minute dwell. A school asked for a later arrival, but no change has been approved.
## Lakeside Spur
The Lakeside Spur covers 21 kilometres and visits three stops. It needs 3.0 driver hours. One bridge has a posted 7.5-tonne limit, and the source does not state the vehicle's loaded weight.
## Recorded comparison
North Loop has the longest list of stops among the two rural options, Market Link has the most stops overall, and Lakeside Spur has the longest distance. These measures describe the route sheet only; they do not establish which route serves more readers.
## Trial review
The trial coordinator will bring mileage, visit counts, and missed-stop reports to the 9 October 2027 review. No route cost or attendance forecast appears in this note.

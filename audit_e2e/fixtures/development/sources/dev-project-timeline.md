# Flood Sensor Installation Schedule
## Overview
The city plans staged sensor installation at four canal gates.
## Scope
The city drainage office plans a staged installation of level sensors at four canal gates. The dates below are planning dates, not confirmed completion records.
## Survey
Field survey begins 6 April 2027 and is scheduled to finish 9 April. The survey crew checks mounting clearance and cellular signal at each gate.
## Procurement
The purchasing team plans to issue the equipment order on 16 April. The supplier lead time is listed as 18 calendar days in the planning note.
## Bench test
The controls team reserves 10 and 11 May for a bench test of the four sensor units and the alarm dashboard.
## Field installation
Installation is scheduled for 17–20 May, one gate per day. The plan assigns two technicians and one traffic marshal to each field shift.
## Acceptance review
The office plans an acceptance review on 28 May. It will check message delivery, timestamp agreement, and the manual-gauge comparison; the source gives no pass threshold.

# Community Energy Survey Extract
## Overview
Twelve months of fictional energy survey data are supplied for table-layout testing.
## What this extract contains
This fictional worksheet contains twelve monthly rows from a demonstration energy survey. Values are supplied for table-layout testing and do not describe a real utility or customer.

## Reading the columns
Sites counts locations in the monthly sample. Meter reads counts received readings. Median kWh/site is an energy quantity per site; late reads counts records received after the monthly close.
## Coverage caveat
The number of sites changes during the year, and the extract does not identify which sites enter or leave. Month-to-month medians therefore should not be read as a fixed panel comparison.
## Source label
Every row carries a DEMO batch label. These labels identify fictional fixture rows and cannot be used to locate an external data source.

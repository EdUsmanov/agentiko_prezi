# Library Room Booking Demonstration
## Source note
This fictional monthly series is supplied for chart and workbook-resource layout testing. It does not describe real library bookings.
## Bookings
The demo sheet B-01 lists 72 bookings in January, 81 in February, 93 in March, 88 in April, 104 in May, and 111 in June 2027.
## Attendance
The separate demo sheet B-02 lists 486 attendee visits in January, 530 in February, 601 in March, 574 in April, 688 in May, and 720 in June.
## Cancellation count
The demo sheet B-03 lists 8 cancelled bookings in January, 11 in February, 9 in March, 13 in April, 12 in May, and 10 in June.
## Units and relationship
Bookings and cancellations are event counts. Attendee visits count people entering a room, so one booking may include several visits. The series should not share a single unlabelled unit.
## Provenance limits
Each sheet is generated fixture data with a B-01, B-02, or B-03 label. It contains no workbook export from an external organization.

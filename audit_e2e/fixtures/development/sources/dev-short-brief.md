# Neighborhood Repair Pilot
## Need
The Riverside public library receives repeated requests for basic bicycle repairs, but its current events offer no repair service.
## Offer
The library will host a weekly repair table in the ground-floor workshop during a ten-week pilot.
## Capacity
Two volunteer mechanics will attend each Saturday session and may accept at most twelve bicycles per day.
## Measures
The coordinator will record completed repairs, items declined, and whether a visitor returns within thirty days.
## Decision
The library board reviews the ten-week results on 18 June 2027 and decides whether to continue the service.
